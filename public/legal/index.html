Free Pilot notice

These provisional statements explain the current product boundaries. A lawyer-approved terms of service and privacy notice, verified business identity and POPIA review are required before wider public or commercial access.

What the pilot does

Approved accounts can access private rooms, send human messages, create rooms and invite other previously enrolled accounts. Room limits and authorization are checked by the existing Supabase project.

What it does not do

Public account registration, paid checkout, unlimited AI, automatic AI posting, evidence release and Sparks are disabled. This page is not a consent record for AI processing.

Your data and hosting

Firebase would serve static frontend files only; sign-in, user accounts, messages and room membership remain with the existing Supabase backend. WATT does not claim all data is stored in South Africa; cross-border processing needs a proper assessment.

Important limitations

Sessions are memory-only and end upon reload or expiration. No permanent browser storage of access tokens is used by this application. Do not post sensitive personal information until the final terms, policies and security review are approved.
